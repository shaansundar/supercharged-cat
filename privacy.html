Privacy Policy

supercharged_cat · Last updated 21 October 2026

supercharged_cat is a private command-line tool, operated by the owner of the YouTube channel CAT Prep Supercharged, that uploads the channel's educational videos to it, organises them into playlists, and reads how long viewers watch those videos. It runs only on the operator's own computer and is used only by the operator, with the operator's own Google account. This policy explains what the tool accesses through Google and YouTube, and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

supercharged_cat uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

When the account owner signs in, the tool asks for the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube (manage your YouTube account). It uses that permission only to:

  • read the signed-in channel's ID and name, to confirm it is posting to the right channel;
  • upload the operator's own videos with their title, description, tags, category, visibility and content disclosures;
  • read back the status of videos it has just uploaded;
  • list the channel's playlists, create a playlist when one is missing, and add uploaded videos to it;
  • set a custom thumbnail image on videos it has uploaded;
  • update the description of videos it has uploaded, to add links between the channel's related videos.

It also asks for https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly (view YouTube Analytics reports for your content). It uses that permission only to read, for the channel's own uploaded videos: the audience-retention curve; totals of views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, likes, comments, shares, and subscribers gained and lost; the same numbers per day; and the share of views from each traffic source (such as the Shorts feed or search). These are aggregate numbers from YouTube Analytics; no individual viewer is identified.

It also asks for https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (see, edit and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions), because it is the permission YouTube requires for uploading captions. It uses that permission only to upload a closed-caption track to videos it has uploaded, made from the video's own narration, and to list the caption tracks on those videos so a track is not added twice. It does not use it to read, edit or delete ratings, comments, or any other content.

It does not read, collect or change anything else in the account, such as comments, subscribers, watch history, messages or other people's data.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens. The access and refresh tokens Google issues are saved in a file on the operator's computer that only the operator's user account can read. They are never sent anywhere except back to Google to authorise requests.
  • Upload records. The ID, link, upload time, visibility and playlist of each uploaded video are saved in local project files, so the same video is not uploaded twice.
  • Analytics snapshots. The analytics numbers above are saved per video in local project files, one snapshot each time they are read so their change over time can be measured, and used only to improve how the channel's future videos are written.

The tool has no servers, databases or analytics of its own. It sets no cookies and shows no advertising.

Sharing

Information received from Google APIs is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. The only data sent anywhere is what goes to Google and YouTube to carry out the actions listed above.

supercharged_cat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the token file is deleted. Upload records and analytics snapshots are kept as a log of the channel's videos and can be deleted at any time.

You can revoke supercharged_cat's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. Once revoked, the stored tokens stop working.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by its operator.
