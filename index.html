supercharged_cat

The upload tool behind the CAT Prep Supercharged YouTube channel

CAT Prep Supercharged makes short, graphics-led videos that solve previous-year Quant, Logical Reasoning and Data Interpretation questions for the CAT, XAT and GMAT exams, one question per Short.

supercharged_cat is the private tool that posts those videos. Once a video is finished, it uploads the video to the channel with its title, description and tags through the YouTube API Services, then adds it to the right playlist, such as "CAT Quant PYQs". It runs on the operator's own computer, with the channel owner's own Google account. It is not a public service and has no other users.
